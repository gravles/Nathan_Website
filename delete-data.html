FitnessTracker

Delete My Account & Data

Submit a request to permanently delete your account and all associated data from FitnessTracker.

← Privacy Policy

Your data belongs to you. Once your request is received, your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted within 30 days. This action cannot be undone.

How to submit your request

1
Click the button below to open a pre-filled email request. Your email client will open with the subject and body already filled in.
2
Verify the email address matches the one you used to sign up for FitnessTracker, then send.
3
You will receive a confirmation reply within 2 business days. Deletion will be completed within 30 days of confirmation.
✉  Send Deletion Request

Prefer to write your own email? Send to nathandavie@gmail.com with subject "FitnessTracker – Account & Data Deletion Request" and include the email address on your account.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and login credentials
  • All daily logs (weight, sleep, mood, energy, steps)
  • All workout history, programs, and personal records
  • All nutrition data, meal plans, and pantry items
  • All progress photos
  • All body measurements and metrics history
  • Push notification tokens and reminder settings
  • All AI coach conversation history
  • Any connected Strava integration data

Retention note: Anonymised aggregate statistics (e.g. total number of app users) that cannot be linked back to you are not subject to deletion. No other data is retained after your request is processed.
